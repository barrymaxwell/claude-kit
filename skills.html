Sessions

/where

Shows where this session is working.

Shows
Repo, worktree, folder, sync state, uncommitted files, dev server, last commit and PR.
Changes
Nothing. Read-only.
/recap

Sums up the session's work.

Shows
What was done, by session. Worktrees and branches, dev servers, links, and anything still open.
Changes
Nothing. Read-only.
Option
/recap onboarding only narrows the Done list.
/close

Cleans up after the session.

Checks
Uncommitted, unpushed or unmerged work in this session and the sessions it started.
Changes
Removes merged worktrees and branches. Stops their dev servers. Keeps anything unsaved and asks.
Leaves
Other sessions' worktrees and servers, and the sessions themselves.
/branches

Lists every open branch and worktree across your repos.

Shows
The Branch Board artifact, refreshed, plus a few lines on what needs you.
Option
/branches Telatris filters to one repo or folder.
